Help for the iPhone, iPad and Android app

How to play

Slide your finger left and right to move the paddle. Tap to launch the ball. Break every brick to clear the level, catch power-ups, build combos and beat the boss. Tap the pause button to pause.

My scores disappeared

Scores and settings are saved on your device only. They are removed if you delete the app or clear its data, and they don't move between devices.

There is no sound

Check that SFX and Music are turned on in the main menu or in Settings, that your device's volume is up, and on iPhone that the silent switch is off.

The game feels slow

Close other apps and restart the game. Choosing Easy difficulty in Settings also slows the ball down.

Contact us

Found a bug or have an idea? Email support@homefilterapp.tech and tell us your device model and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.
