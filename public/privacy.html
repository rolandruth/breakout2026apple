Privacy Policy

Breakout 2026 · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Breakout 2026 ("the app") is an arcade game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on iPhone, iPad or Android.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Your high scores, chosen difficulty, unlocked levels, skins and sound settings are saved only on your own device so the game remembers them. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Tracking and advertising

The app contains no advertising and no tracking. We do not use your information to track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share any information with third parties.

Internet use

The game is fully playable offline. When your device is online, the app may contact our game server (hosted by Base44) to check that the game is available. This request contains no personal information. Like any internet service, the server may briefly process your device's IP address to deliver the response; it is not used to identify you.

Sharing your score

If you use the Share button, the app creates a score image and hands it to your device's own sharing menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
